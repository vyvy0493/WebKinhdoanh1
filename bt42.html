Run the Messier Marathon

It's time for the annual Messier Marathon! Instead of facing up their noses, amateur astronomers will be heading telescopes, star charts, and a hefty pot of coffee in an all-night vigil chasing the Messier objects.

Messier objects are stellar objects, classified by astronomer Charles Messier in the 18th century, ranging from diffuse galaxies to star clusters to nebulae.

March is the only month of the year in which all 110 Messier objects are in the sky.

Unfortunately, if you want to see all of them, you have to start looking right after sunset and continue until just before sunrise.

Ironically, Charles Messier wasn't all that interested in his objects. He made the list in order to avoid seeing them because Messier was more interested in discovering new comets.
